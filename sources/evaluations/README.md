# Evaluation sources

This directory contains lifecycle-v0 fixture implementations, frozen execution contracts, retained compact provider-run evidence, derived comparisons, and audit receipts.

- `fixtures/` — pinned Fastify, Beets, and Terraform lifecycle lanes.
- `protocols/` — immutable frozen execution contracts. Their embedded readiness text records freeze-time state; current execution state lives in `data/workflow-sessions.json`.
- `workflow-sessions/` — retained four-file compact bundles for accepted operational runs plus co-located derived matched baseline/treatment comparison JSON; cumulative-accounting corrections supersede legacy totals copied from historical registry records.
- `audits/` — qualification, accounting, deletion, installation-parity, actual-use, and aggregate-analysis receipts.

The active registry contains 100 accepted provider-backed sessions: 34 controls and 66 eligible individual-tool treatments. Invalid historical treatments were deleted—not relabelled as baseline—under explicit experiment-owner receipts. The current Phase 2 synthesis is derived from `audits/phase-2-corrected-analysis-20260720.json`; later natural-use, assisted-v1, Baseline V3, and Baseline V4 records retain their own frozen populations.

Baseline V3 is the retained low-complexity control design. Its provider-free qualification receipts and frozen pilot contracts are indexed by `audits/baseline-v3-task-family-qualification-20260722.json`; its three immutable paid-attempt receipts, recovered compact sessions, matrix evidence, and zero-incident decision are retained alongside that qualification record. The 2026-07-22 pilot used 236,151 provider tokens. All nine model commands exited zero, while the repeated controller verifier passed Fastify 3/3 and reported Beets and Terraform 0/3 because their wrapper omitted `WORKFLOW_REPO`. The original matrix publication rolled back on two stale repository-test assumptions; the tests were corrected and the exact compact bundles were recovered without provider reruns. All three V3 pilot identities are occupied. The failed Baseline V2 attempt remains immutable at `audits/baseline-v2-pilot-zero-mistake.json`, with redacted surviving evidence under `audits/baseline-v2-pilot-20260722-failed/`. Fastify remains V3 treatment-gate eligible; Beets and Terraform V3 remain permanently blocked.

Baseline V4 is active only for Beets and Terraform. Their authorized r0 samples, `baseline-beets-20260728-p-fcc8438d2077-r0` and `baseline-terraform-20260728-p-5811b463c1e9-r0`, retain 88,200 and 87,811 provider tokens respectively; all six command exits and repeated verifiers passed, and all eight independently reviewed incident counts are zero for both. `audits/baseline-v4-pilot-zero-mistake.json` records the two sequence-level passes, while `audits/baseline-v4-pilot-20260728/` and `audits/baseline-v4-pilot-terraform-20260728/` preserve each original rollback and provider-free recovery. Both sequences are eligible for treatment-protocol freeze, both pilot identities are non-rerunnable, and no V4 treatment protocol exists. `audits/current-low-complexity-control-token-cost-20260728.json` reports the valid mixed-generation controls using `fresh + 0.1×cached + 6×output`. The serialized r1 samples are retained and immutable: Fastify 90,463, Beets 87,396, and Terraform 87,816 provider tokens. r2 retained zero-incident Fastify and Terraform controls at 90,420 and 87,784 tokens. Beets r2 is an occupied pre-provider controller failure with zero spend and no session. `audits/current-low-complexity-baseline-r2-partial-recovery-20260728/` preserves the two valid sessions' audits, the Beets failure disposition, and provider-free recovery. Any replacement Beets identity requires fresh experiment-owner authorization.

Provider-free qualification receipts prove setup, warm state, assignment, concealment, and applicable MCP handshakes for their exact frozen protocol hashes. They do not prove provider execution, natural uptake, or product effect. Accepted provider-backed evidence lives in the compact session bundles and current registry.

Four compact bundles carry a `source-diff-generated-state-exclusion-v1` repair receipt: the three Graphify lanes exclude `graphify-out`, and CodeScope Fastify excludes `.fastembed_cache` and `.codescope`. Those paths are generated treatment state, not source checkpoints. Repaired `run.json` records preserve the original artifact hashes, sizes, and removed-section counts; manifests hash the corrected compact bytes.
