# Evaluation sources

This directory contains Lifecycle V1 fixture implementations, frozen execution contracts, retained compact provider-run evidence, derived comparisons, and audit receipts.

- `fixtures/` — pinned Fastify, Beets, and Terraform lifecycle lanes; only Fastify and Beets are active in Lifecycle V1.
- `protocols/` — immutable frozen execution contracts. Their embedded readiness text records freeze-time state; current execution state lives in `data/workflow-sessions.json`.
- `workflow-sessions/` — retained four-file compact bundles for accepted operational runs plus co-located derived matched baseline/treatment comparison JSON; cumulative-accounting corrections supersede legacy totals copied from historical registry records.
- `audits/` — qualification, accounting, deletion, installation-parity, actual-use, and aggregate-analysis receipts.

The registry contains 103 accepted provider-backed Lifecycle V1 sessions: 18 baselines, 4 replacement-runtime controls, and 81 individual-tool treatments. Lifecycle V0 was retired on 2026-08-14 under `audits/lifecycle-v0-framework-retired-20260814.json`; its sessions, artifacts, and protocols are deleted and are not comparison controls. Invalid historical treatments were deleted—not relabelled as baseline—under explicit experiment-owner receipts. Lifecycle V1 is active only for Fastify and Beets: provider-free qualification passed component and project-wide compilation, each active lane retains a bare-Codex GPT-5.6 Sol/`high` r0 pilot, and the direct-Anthropic Claude Code r0 baseline pairs are complete. Thirteen Fastify and two Beets Sonnet treatment lanes, plus two RepoWise Codex V2 lanes, are retained. Fastify/Cartog and Fastify/TokenJuice are occupied failures excluded from the registry and aggregate claims, while thirteen Beets Claude lanes remain unlaunched. Terraform V1's owner-declared-invalid r0 was removed under `audits/lifecycle-v1-terraform-invalidated-20260802.json` and has no active rerun or treatment path. The current Phase 2 synthesis is derived from `audits/phase-2-corrected-analysis-20260720.json`; later natural-use, assisted-v1, Baseline V3, Baseline V4, OpenCode, Claude Code, and RepoWise records retain their own frozen populations.

RepoWise 0.39.0 has two provider-configured qualification/preparation receipts covering Codex and OpenCode on Fastify and Beets. Preparation makes no provider calls and is not an effectiveness result. The Codex V2 screen now retains two provider-backed sessions and matched comparisons; the OpenCode protocols remain unexecuted. Provider-free qualification receipts prove setup, warm state, assignment, concealment, and applicable MCP handshakes for their exact frozen protocol hashes. They do not prove provider execution, natural uptake, or product effect. Accepted provider-backed evidence lives in the compact session bundles and current registry.

`audits/opencode-next-five-sol-high-r1-results-20260730.json` publishes the completed fresh-bare plus CodeScope, SwarmVault, Graphify, RTK, and CodeGraph r1 screen: 18/18 sessions, 54/54 task verifiers, 901,585 total provider tokens, and 432,508.4 weighted units. It reconciles 108 native usage events, all compact manifests and frozen protocols, 15 same-workflow comparisons, activation versus uptake, task-target versus full-tree parity, product state, and retry exclusions.

`audits/opencode-next-five-batch2-results-20260730.json` publishes the successive corrected jCodemunch, LeanCTX, SigMap, Caveman, and LowFat r1 screen. The 15 treatment sessions retained 1,098,566 raw provider tokens and 472,615.4 weighted units, with 43/45 task verifiers and 14/15 final verifiers passing. The audit reconciles every final cumulative usage event, all 15 manifests, 840 evidence members, model-issued product uptake, and matched comparisons.
