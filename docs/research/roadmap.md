## Production evidence now retained

1. Every accepted session is bound to a frozen Lifecycle V1 protocol and compatible baseline pool.
2. Every corrected profile passed provider-free setup, warm-state, assignment, concealment, and applicable MCP handshake gates before provider execution.
3. Accepted compact manifests verify, and isolation audits pass.
4. Provider accounting uses one final monotonic cumulative snapshot per distinct thread; older records are interpreted through the correction audit.
5. Token eligibility is independent of verifier outcome; correctness remains diagnostic and did not trigger pass-selection reruns.
6. Retired-generation results are preserved only in the retirement receipt and in annotated papers; they are not active findings.

## Next research step

Do not rerun occupied natural-use samples to replace unfavorable, high-variance, or near-neutral results. New provider work must be prospective, separately indexed, and explicitly authorized.

1. Preserve every Lifecycle V1 task, qualification, protocol, receipt, compact session, and audit identity exactly; never rewrite or rerun an occupied historical identity.
2. Do not authorize a Lifecycle V1 rerun or treatment while the active Fastify/Beets pilot audit remains pending; Terraform V1 has no active lane, and its invalid r0 was removed rather than reopened.
3. Retain the first operationally valid Lifecycle V1 sample per active sequence and require all component compile outcomes plus final project compilation to pass before treatment protocol freeze.
4. Give any authorized treatment and its compatible control identical task-assistance bytes while preserving product-authored treatment guidance and forbidding evaluator-authored tool steering.
5. Retain the three-task persistent-session structure and first-valid-sample policy.
6. Keep treatment telemetry observational; do not force tool uptake.
7. Preregister any future stack under a new identity.

Lifecycle V1 is the only framework that answers the question this repository asks. Retired generations are not alternative lenses on the same question; see the retirement receipt for why their estimand was confounded.
