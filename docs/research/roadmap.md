# Research roadmap

## Current state

The official-integration repair and first-valid individual-tool screen are complete. Fastify, Beets, and Terraform retain three eligible GPT-5.6 Luna/`xhigh` bare-Codex baselines each (`r0`–`r2`), and a separate Sol/`high` baseline panel contributes nine additional controls. The active corpus contains 18 controls and 51 eligible individual-tool treatment sessions across 17 conditions. Invalid historical sessions were deleted rather than relabelled as baseline, with explicit receipts preserving the adjudications.

The Phase 2 analysis uses final cumulative provider usage per thread. Across all 17 matched assignments, treatments retained 582,180,587 provider tokens against 541,295,326 repeated matched-baseline tokens, a descriptive 7.55% increase, with 150/153 task verifiers. Five conditions were lower in aggregate; only TokenJuice (-22.28%) and SigMap (-9.60%) were lower on Fastify, Beets, and Terraform. These are one-sample reproduction observations, not stable rankings.

The guide-faithful `retrieval-jcodemunch-codex-mcp-v2` supersedes deleted direct v1 and now has a valid provider-backed sample. The corrected canonical CodeGraph v1 generation is distinguished from its deleted predecessor by frozen protocol hashes; retained evidence proves MCP initialization and 23 successful model-issued `codegraph explore` calls across all nine tasks. Ponytail and Caveman likewise use their author-documented Codex plugin/skill surfaces rather than the deleted prompt emulations. The historical TokenJuice+jcodemunch stack decision remains withdrawn.

## Next research step

Do not rerun occupied first-valid samples to replace unfavorable or near-neutral results. New provider work must be prospective, separately indexed, and explicitly authorized.

1. Replicate TokenJuice and SigMap first because both were lower on all three lanes with 9/9 verifier outcomes.
2. Replicate Ponytail to test whether its large but lane-mixed aggregate reduction is stable.
3. Use RTK, Cartog, CodeGraph, and jcodemunch as near-neutral controls when designing replication power and mechanism instrumentation.
4. Retain the negative first samples for higher-token treatments; do not outcome-select them away.
5. Add treatment-appropriate telemetry for hooks, wrappers, proxies, MCP calls, turn counts, and setup latency without forcing model uptake.
6. Preregister any stack as a new identity. Valid individual evidence does not revive the deleted historical stack or justify adding individual percentages.

The original provider-free qualification receipts remain evidence for their exact frozen hashes. They prove setup and readiness only; the current product-effect observations are owned by accepted sessions, the cumulative accounting audit, and the corrected Phase 2 analysis.
